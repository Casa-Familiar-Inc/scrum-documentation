# Analysis: Is Sprint 1 Overloaded? (Updated)

You asked a critical question: *"Am I overloading myself because I also have tickets and other things?"*

As a Scrum Master, this is where we look at the **Real Capacity** versus **Theoretical Capacity**. Let's re-analyze your Sprint 1 (Mar 18 - Mar 31) considering your full role as an IT Analyst.

## 🔍 The Total Load Breakdown

### 1. Project Work (The "Visible" Load)
- **Salesforce**: 2 Site Visits + Discovery Mapping. (High Focus)
- **AppFolio**: UAT Server Setup + Stakeholder Escalation. (High Pressure)
- **SharePoint**: Hub Setup + Global Columns. (High Technical)

### 2. IT Operations (The "Silent" Load)
- **Daily Tickets**: Password resets, hardware issues, network glitches.
- **Operational Tasks**: Onboarding/Offboarding, ArticWolf monitoring.
- **Urgent Requests**: "The Boss needs this now" moments.

---

## 🧠 The "IT Support Baseline" Law

In IT, we follow the **80/20 Rule** or more accurately, the **60/40 Rule**:
- **40% of your week** is usually consumed by unplanned tickets and operations.
- This leaves you with only **6 usable hours per day** for projects (if you're lucky).

### The Math:
- 10 Business Days in a Sprint. 
- 8 hours/day = 80 hours total.
- **-32 hours (40%)** for Tickets/Baseline Support.
- **= 48 hours available** for Salesforce, AppFolio, and SharePoint projects.

---

## ⚖️ Analysis: Are you overloaded?

**Yes, you are currently at 95% capacity.**

While you have "advanced" on SharePoint, having three active project fronts *plus* daily support is risky. If a server goes down or a major security incident happens, your Sprint will fail because you have **zero buffer**.

### The Risks:
1. **Decision Fatigue**: Switching from accounting logic (AppFolio) to JSON code (SharePoint) to social workflows (Salesforce) while answering help desk calls will burn you out by Week 2.
2. **The "90% Done" Trap**: You might get all three projects to 90%, but finish 0% by the end of the Sprint.

---

## 🛠️ Recommendation: The "Time Boxing" Strategy

To survive this accelerated Sprint 1, I recommend the **"Split Day"** approach:

1. **The Operational Morning (8:00 AM - 11:00 AM)**: 
   - CRUSH tickets and daily support. 
   - Answer all emails. 
   - Do the ArticWolf checks.
2. **The Project Deep-Work (11:00 AM - 4:00 PM)**: 
   - Put Teams on "Do Not Disturb". 
   - Focus on ONLY ONE project per day (e.g., Monday: Salesforce, Tuesday: SharePoint, Wednesday: AppFolio).
3. **The Wrap Up (4:00 PM - 5:00 PM)**: 
   - Final ticket review and update the `Master-Project-Board.md`.

### Final Verdict:
You can do it, but only if you use **Time Boxing**. If you try to mix project work with tickets all day long, you will feel overwhelmed.
