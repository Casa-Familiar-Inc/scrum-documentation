# 📊 Salesforce Reporting & KPI Project

## 🏢 Organizational Context
Casa Familiar (San Ysidro, CA) is integrating **Social Services (SS)** and **Financial Opportunity Center (FOC)** processes using the **Salesforce** platform. **Cloud Care** is the consulting firm responsible for creating custom 'intakes' within Salesforce for data capture. The primary goal of this project is to consolidate data to generate effective reports and measure Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).

## 👥 Stakeholders
- **Nefi Lopez**: IT Analyst / Scrum Master
- **Gema**: Social Services Administrator (Immigration, Assistance with Procedures)
- **Mayra**: FOC Administrator (Financial Services)
- **Cloud Care**: Salesforce Implementation Consulting
- **IT Leadership**: Project Sponsor

## 🎯 Main Objectives
1. Understand the current workflow of how Gema and Mayra generate their reports.
2. Evaluate the effectiveness of the custom intakes created by Cloud Care.
3. Establish measurable KPIs that Salesforce can present automatically.
4. Identify additional "Low Code" automation opportunities to ease the administrative burden on the centers.

## 📈 Agile Methodology
We will work with a simplified **Agile-Lite** approach, focusing on weekly priorities and measurable milestones for the executive team.
