# Analysis: When should our Sprints start?

You asked a brilliant question: *Should we start Sprints on Tuesday or Wednesday?* 

In traditional Scrum, teams often default to Monday-to-Friday. However, in the real world (especially in IT Support and Non-Profits like Casa Familiar), starting on a Monday is usually a bad idea.

Here is a breakdown of why choosing a mid-week start is highly recommended, and **why Wednesday is the absolute best choice.**

## Why NOT Monday or Friday?

### The "Monday Madness"
- Mondays are for catching up on emails from the weekend.
- Unplanned urgent requests from the Boss usually happen on Monday morning.
- If you schedule **Sprint Planning** on a Monday, it gets constantly interrupted or delayed.

### The "Friday Fatigue"
- Energy levels are lowest on Friday afternoons.
- If a Sprint ends on a Friday, people often rush through their final tasks (reducing quality) just to go home.
- If you have an urgent deployment at the end of the Sprint, doing it on a Friday risks breaking things over the weekend.

## 🤝 The Contenders: Tuesday vs. Wednesday

### Option 1: Tuesday to Monday Sprints
- **Start**: Tuesday (Sprint Planning).
- **End**: Monday (Sprint Review / Retrospective).
- **Pros**: You avoid the Monday morning rush to plan.
- **Cons**: The Sprint *ends* on a Monday. This means if you are behind on your tasks, you will feel pressured to work over the weekend to finish them before the Monday Review. This leads to burnout.

### Option 2: Wednesday to Tuesday Sprints (Highly Recommended)
- **Start**: Wednesday (Sprint Planning).
- **End**: Tuesday (Sprint Review / Retrospective).
- **Pros**: 
  1. **Maximum Focus**: By Wednesday, the "Monday Madness" is over. You can plan with a clear head.
  2. **Safe Weekends**: If you are behind on Friday, you still have Monday and Tuesday to finish your Sprint tasks without working the weekend!
  3. **Holiday Proof**: Most public holidays (3-day weekends) fall on a Monday or Friday. A Wednesday-Tuesday sprint is almost never interrupted by a long weekend holiday.
- **Cons**: It feels slightly unnatural at first if you are used to a normal work-week calendar, but the benefits quickly outweigh this.
