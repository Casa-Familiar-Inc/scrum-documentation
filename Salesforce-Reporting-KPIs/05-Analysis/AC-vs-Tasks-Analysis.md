# 📝 Analysis: Acceptance Criteria (AC) vs. Tasks

It is very common to confuse **Acceptance Criteria** with **Tasks**. In an Agile/Scrum environment, they serve different purposes: one defines the **WHAT** & **WHY** (Quality), and the other defines the **HOW** (Execution).

## 🧐 Comparison Table

| Feature | Acceptance Criteria (AC) | Tasks |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Focus** | **The Goal / Result** (What the boss sees). | **The Action** (What you actually do). |
| **Viewpoint** | "The requirement is met IF..." | "I need to do X, then Y..." |
| **Ownership** | Shared with Stakeholders (Gema/Mayra). | Internal to the Developer/Analyst (Nefi). |
| **Example** | "The report must show total intakes per month." | "Export CSV from Salesforce to Excel." |
| **Done?** | Defines when the User Story is *finished*. | Defines the individual steps to get there. |

---

## 💡 Practical Example for your Visit

### **User Story**: Visit Gema to understand Social Services Intakes.

#### **Acceptance Criteria (The "What" is required to finish)**
> *The visit is successful ONLY if:*
- [ ] We have a clear diagram of the current manual process.
- [ ] We identified all missing fields in Salesforce compared to their Excel.
- [ ] Gema agrees on the primary KPI to be tracked.

#### **Tasks (The "How" you get it done)**
> *The steps Nefi needs to take:*
1. Send Outlook invite to Gema.
2. Prepare the recording/notebook for the session.
3. Ask Gema to show her screen while doing an intake.
4. Take a screenshot of the legacy Excel header.

---

## 🚀 Why distinguish them?

1. **For the Boss**: Your boss usually cares about the **Acceptance Criteria**. They want to know: "Did we define the KPIs?" (Yes/No).
2. **For You**: You care about the **Tasks**. They help you manage your time: "I have 5 things to do today to complete that visit story."
3. **Clarity**: It prevents you from getting lost in details. If you finish all the tasks but the Acceptance Criteria isn't met (e.g., you visited Gema but forgot to ask about the KPIs), the Story is **NOT Done**.

## 🛠️ Recommendation for Nefi
In your `01-Backlog.md`, keep both:
- Use **Acceptance Criteria** to confirm you've met the business goal.
- Use **Tasks** as your personal checklist to move the needle every day.
