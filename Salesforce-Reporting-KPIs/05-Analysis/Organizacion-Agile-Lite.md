# 🚀 Agile-Lite Organization: Better Order, Less Complexity

If strict Scrum feels too heavy, we can use a **Hybrid** approach. The goal is for your boss to see constant progress without you being trapped in excessive ceremonies.

## 🛠️ How to use your SharePoint Columns (Lite Version)

| SharePoint Column | Suggested "Lite" Use | Why it helps |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Category** | **Project Name** | Groups everything for "Salesforce", "IT Support", or "Infrastructure". |
| **Progress** | **Simple Status** | Use only: `💡 Idea`, `⏳ Backlog`, `🏃 Doing`, `✅ Done`. |
| **Priority** | **Impact** | `🔥 Critical`, `⭐ Important`, `☁️ Optional/Low`. |
| **Notes** | **Weekly Update** | Write the latest progress here; it serves as the base for your report. |

## 📂 Obsidian Project Organization

Instead of complex Sprints, I suggest organizing your "Workbench" in Obsidian like this:

### 1. The "Master Board" (Eagle Eye View)
A single file summarizing where you are today (Refer to `Master-Project-Board.md`).

### 2. The "3 Daily Tasks" Method
To avoid being overwhelmed by 20+ tasks:
- Choose the **3 tasks** with the highest impact today.
- Once finished, move to the rest. This allows you to report "daily wins" to your boss.

## 📊 Advantages for your Executive Report
With this simplified organization, your report to the boss changes from:
*"I'm working on many things"*
To:
*"This week I finished the 3 highest-priority tasks for Salesforce, moving the project 15% closer to the goal. Next week my focus shifts to FOC."*
