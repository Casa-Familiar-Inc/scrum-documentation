# Fall Festival 2026

## Event Description

The Fall Festival is a free, community-centered event hosted by Casa Familiar at the San Ysidro Civic Center. The festival offers a family-friendly experience featuring a pumpkin patch, food vendors, and seasonal activities celebrating the fall season. This is a walk-in event open to the San Ysidro community and surrounding areas -- no registration required.

> [!NOTE]
> This event is in **early planning**. Many details below are placeholders (marked TBD) and will be updated as the committee is assembled and logistics are finalized.
