# Abrazo 5K Walk & 10K Run 2026

## Event Description

The Abrazo 5K Walk & 10K Run takes participants along the scenic San Ysidro-Tijuana border. This year's theme is **"Get Up, Stand Up"**, inspired by Bob Marley's words: *"One love, one heart, let's get together and feel all right."* We honor his legacy of using music to uplift voices, champion social justice, and spread messages of love, unity, and resilience.

Casa Familiar continues this spirit through our mission of **Advancing Communities** -- even in the face of adversity -- by celebrating the strength of our comunidad. Join us to uplift community wins and support Casa's youth and senior programs. As a participant, you'll help us meet growing needs while celebrating together.

Expect lively **Rasta vibes**, energizing music, and two special awards presented after the race during a short closing program.
