# Strategy: How to handle the Unresponsive Stakeholder

As the Project Manager/Coordinator for the AppFolio-QuickBooks integration, your biggest current blocker is the internal stakeholder who needs to verify the accounting data. They haven't responded to emails or Teams in over a week. 

In IT Project Management, you cannot let another person's silence delay your project without a paper trail. Here is a professional, escalated strategy to unblock this.

## The Escalation Ladder

### 1. The "Assumption of Approval" Email (The PM's Best Trick)
When people don't respond to "Can you verify this?", you flip the script. You give them a deadline, after which you assume they approve. This forces them to answer if they see a risk.
- **Draft to use:**
  > *"Hi [Name], I'm checking in on the data verification for Dancing Numbers. They are currently completely blocked on development until we confirm the lists are accurate. If I don't hear back from you by **Wednesday at 3:00 PM**, we will assume the data is 100% correct and instruct the vendor to proceed to the next phase. Please let me know if you need more time."*

### 2. The 15-Minute "Ambush" Meeting
Accounting people are notoriously busy and hate writing long emails to verify things. Make it easy for them.
- **Action:** Send a calendar invite for a 15-minute call.
- **Description:** *"Dancing Numbers Blocker - Quick Verbal Yes/No needed on data accuracy."*
- **Outcome:** Get them on the phone, ask the question, write down their answer, and immediately post it in the Teams chat so there is a record.

### 3. The "CC The Boss" Escalation
If Steps 1 and 2 fail, it becomes an organizational risk. The vendor's timeline is slipping, and it's not IT's fault.
- **Action:** Reply to the original email thread.
- **CC:** Your Boss (IT Director) and their Boss (CFO / Finance Director).
- **Draft:** *"Hi [Name], bringing [Bosses' Names] into the loop. Dancing Numbers has been officially stalled for 8 days waiting on this accounting verification. To keep the project timeline intact, how can IT help facilitate this review for you today?"*

## Key PM Rule for Nefi
**Your job isn't to verify the accounting data. Your job is to verify that *they* verified it.**
Never take responsibility for the accuracy of financial numbers. Guard your UAT server, maintain your Tailscale access, and use the communication strategies above to force the blockers out of your way.
